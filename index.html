How to use — FarmMeasure

🌐 Regional languages: switch between English and हिन्दी from Languages (☰ menu on mobile, 🌐 button on desktop). Reports print in the same language.

1. Plot your field

  1. Tap ⬟ Draw polygon (top-left) to start.
  2. Tap corners of your field on the map. For uneven edges, add more points.
  3. Close the shape: tap the first point or press Enter / double-tap.
  4. Drag points or the shape to adjust. Use ✎ Edit or 🗑 Delete on each field.
  5. Draw only the area you want measured — leave out ponds, rocky patches, or houses; anything outside your polygon is not counted.

2. Change measurements

  • Standard units (top of panel): m², sq ft, hectares, acres, gaj, are, km², sq mi — govt record truth (ha/acre).
  • Totals and each field update instantly. Secondary line shows 3 other units for quick compare.

3. State-wise units

  • Below the totals, pick State unit e.g. Cent — Andhra Pradesh or Bigha — Rajasthan (Vigha Pucca).
  • Both totals and each field show the converted value. 1 acre = X state units is shown for cross-check with your patwari.
  • Your choice is remembered on this device. Share PDF/GeoJSON — it carries the m² value, so anyone can view in their own units.

4. Map tips

  • Rotate: Hold Shift + scroll, or two-finger twist on mobile, or use the compass (top-left). Tap compass to reset north. Rotation helps align fields to screen.
  • Satellite ↔ Map: Use layer switcher top-right. Satellite shows bunds clearly.
  • Find me: Tap Use my location to center — your location dot appears, not stored.
  • Import: Drag a .geojson/.kml file onto the panel footer or tap Import.
  • Thin bunds: Use the ◐ clarity and HD buttons — top-left on desktop, top-right on mobile — to sharpen faint boundaries. HD uses more data.

All calculations happen on your device. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap / MapTiler. Indicative measurement only — not a legal survey. All values are for reference; whoever generates a report must verify it against revenue records or a licensed surveyor.
